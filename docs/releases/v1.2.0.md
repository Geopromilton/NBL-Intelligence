# NBL-Intelligence 1.2.0

New look across all eleven pages, and a solid 3D model of permeable rock.

## Interface
- New dark "Aqua Verde" design on every page: picture banners, stat cards with icons, light value tiles and clear status badges.
- The top bar keeps only what is used: site switch, search (Ctrl + K), the Stage A chip and the AI status. The AI status card sits at the bottom of the left rail.
- Intelligence: a larger ask panel, short suggested questions, quick-access cards and a side column with site pulse, integrity progress and recent conversations.
- Water plan, Recharge, Scenarios: stat rows, supply split with the rainwater share, a water-balance donut and side-by-side run comparison with Approve and Reject.
- Explore: lettered site markers, every site shown as Unvalidated, confidence layers as separate cards, and a key-insight band taken from the data.
- Integrity: each check in one row with a picture, value tiles per source and the action to take.
- Decisions: counts, source and status filters, coloured status bars, and open actions taken from the integrity checks (no invented suggestions).
- Knowledge: suggested searches, matched words highlighted, file-type icons and passage counts.
- Reports: a "report at a glance" panel and file sizes for generated reports.

## Site twin
- Permeable probability is now drawn as a smooth solid, as in GMS or RockWorks, instead of separate blocks.
  - Solid: a closed body where p is at or above the threshold, coloured by the strongest probability inside it.
  - Shells: three nested translucent surfaces.
  - Cells: the raw M5 model cells, kept for checking.
- Cutaway: cut the model east-west or north-south and read the section face (rock below the threshold in grey-brown, the permeable body in colour).
- Site overview panel, zoom and view buttons, live latitude/longitude and UTM read-out under the cursor, and the volume of rock at or above the threshold (rock volume, not water).

## Unchanged
- Logo and brand assets.
- Engines, data, numbers and labels. All groundwater results stay marked as Stage A pre-drilling scenarios.
